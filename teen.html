How Funngro works for teens

Four steps from sign-up to finished project.

  1. Build your profile

    Show your interests and strengths so companies know what you can do.

  2. Find a project

    Browse categories and choose a project that fits your skills and schedule.

  3. Deliver the work

    Complete the brief the company gave you and submit it.

  4. Learn from the result

    Use what you learned to pick a stronger project next time.

Skills you can develop

Real projects teach more than any one subject. These are the skills teenlancers can practise.

  • Writing
  • Design
  • Social media
  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Time management
  • Working with clients
  • Managing money

Why learning through projects works

You practise, not just read

A project has a goal and a deadline, so you apply a skill instead of only studying it.

You find what you like

Trying different project types helps you see which interests are worth following.

You meet real expectations

Briefs from companies show how professional work is requested and reviewed.

You build a record

Finished work shows what you can do, whatever you choose to study later.
