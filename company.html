How to create a project opportunity

A good brief makes good work possible. Prepare these before you post.

  • Goal: what the finished work should achieve.
  • Scope: what is and is not included.
  • Deadline: when you need it.
  • Format: files, length or channel.
  • Examples: work you like and brand rules.

The project process

From brief to finished work.

  1. Share your brief

    Describe the project, timeline and expected output.

  2. Teens take it on

    Interested teenlancers work on the project through the platform.

  3. Review the work

    Check the delivery against your brief and give feedback.

  4. Close the project

    Confirm the result and decide whether to post another.

How Funngro connects both sides

Companies bring projects. Teenagers bring time, ideas and the wish to learn. Funngro is the platform where the two meet.

Teens see the work on the teen side. Companies describe it here. Details such as pricing, payment and approval steps are set by Funngro, so confirm them on funngro.com.
